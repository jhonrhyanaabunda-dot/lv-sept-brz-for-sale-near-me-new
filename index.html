BRZ vs other Subaru models: how to choose.

Most Subaru shoppers assume every Subaru has all-wheel drive. The BRZ is the exception. It is rear-wheel drive by design, which keeps weight down and steering sharp.

If you want a sporty Subaru with AWD instead, the WRX is the closer match. The comparison below frames the BRZ against two common cross-shops.

06BRZ questions

Frequently asked questions about the BRZ for sale near me.

Does Findlay Subaru have the Subaru BRZ for sale near me in Las Vegas?

We stock the Subaru BRZ at 6455 Roy Horn Way in Las Vegas, NV. BRZ availability changes often because it is a low-volume sport coupe.

Check our new inventory for current units or reach us through the contact page to ask about incoming allocations.

Is the Subaru BRZ all-wheel drive like other Subarus?

No. The BRZ is rear-wheel drive, which sets it apart from most of the Subaru lineup. If you want a sporty Subaru with all-wheel drive, the WRX is the closer match.

Our sales team can show you both so you can feel the difference.

Can I get a manual transmission BRZ?

The BRZ is offered with manual and automatic transmissions. Manual examples often sell first because enthusiast demand stays high. Tell us early if you want a manual, and we will flag the next one that arrives.

Browse current stock at our new inventory page.

What does the BRZ cost and are there any offers?

Pricing and offers change, so we do not publish set numbers here. Ask us for current BRZ pricing and any available incentives, and check our finance page for financing options.

You can also compare market values at kbb.com before you visit.

Should I buy a new or Certified Pre-Owned BRZ?

Both are options. A new BRZ carries the full Subaru new-vehicle warranty, while a Certified Pre-Owned BRZ goes through Subaru's CPO inspection and adds coverage. Findlay Subaru is a Subaru Certified Pre-Owned retailer.

Ask our team to compare the two side by side.

How do I check a used BRZ for recalls?

Run the vehicle's VIN through the federal recall lookup at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It tells you whether any open safety recalls apply.

Our service team can also confirm recall status and complete any open recall work after purchase.
