02How to choose

BRZ vs other Subaru models: how to choose.

Most Subaru shoppers assume every Subaru has all-wheel drive. The BRZ is the exception. It is rear-wheel drive by design, which keeps weight down and steering sharp. If you want a sporty Subaru with AWD instead, the WRX is the closer match.

Is the Subaru BRZ all-wheel drive like other Subarus?

No. The BRZ is rear-wheel drive, which sets it apart from most of the Subaru lineup. If you want a sporty Subaru with all-wheel drive, the WRX is the closer match.

Our sales team can show you both so you can feel the difference.

Should I buy a new or Certified Pre-Owned BRZ?

Both are options. A new BRZ carries the full Subaru new-vehicle warranty, while a Certified Pre-Owned BRZ goes through Subaru's CPO inspection and adds coverage. Findlay Subaru is a Subaru Certified Pre-Owned retailer.

Ask our team to compare the two side by side.

How do I check a used BRZ for recalls?
